scala game development

Snake Game

Created the video game Snake, in which a snake moves across the screen and can turn in every direction. It eats apples to grow, plus a reverse feature that works even after Game Over.

Tetris Game

Created the classic puzzle game Tetris, with different shapes of “Tetrominoes” moving down the screen. You can move and rotate them left and right, as well as drop them to the bottom.

MultiREPL Calculator

Implemented a MultiREPL calculator able to handle a wide range of calculations, from simple addition, subtraction, and multiplication, to factorial equations and simplification with unknown variables.
