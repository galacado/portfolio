OSINT vs. Platform Data

University of Amsterdam · Cybercrime & Forensics

Compared public OSINT with server-side platform data for digital investigations, mapped against EU and Dutch legal thresholds.

osint digital investigations eu & dutch law data access

Overview

This assignment examined the gap between what investigators can learn from open-source intelligence alone, and what is only obtainable through a formal request for server-side platform data — and when each is actually legally accessible.

Approach

  • Compared the evidentiary value and completeness of OSINT-derived data against platform-held data for a representative investigative scenario.
  • Mapped which categories of data require a legal order under EU and Dutch law — such as production orders and data-retention rules — versus what is lawfully accessible as open-source information.
  • Assessed the practical and legal trade-offs investigators face between speed (OSINT) and completeness (platform data).
