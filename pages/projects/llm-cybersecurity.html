LLM Applications in Cybersecurity

Jozef Stefan Institute · Ljubljana, Slovenia

Researching instruction fine-tuning of LLMs for cybersecurity applications.

llm research fine-tuning cybersecurity

In this research internship, I’m exploring the applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) in cybersecurity, with a focus on instruction fine-tuning. The goal is to fine-tune an existing LLM and compare its performance to its non fine-tuned counterpart to assess the impact of fine-tuning.

Our approach is loosely based on the method proposed by Levi et al. (2024).

My responsibilities

  • Creating the instruction fine-tuning dataset.
  • Fine-tuning an LLM using this dataset.
  • Evaluating the performance of the fine-tuned model compared to the non fine-tuned model.

As this project is still ongoing, analysis and results cannot be shared until its completion.
